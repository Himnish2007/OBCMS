Data Logging Interval

How often the OBCMS simulator (or real ingestion service) logs a new reading per axle.

Daily Report Schedule

Every user receives an automatic email report covering only their assigned coaches, once a day at this time (24-hour format, server time).

Email (SMTP) Configuration

Required for real email delivery of alerts and reports. Without this configured, emails are logged only (not actually sent).

SMS Configuration

SMS delivery requires a paid SMS gateway account (e.g. Twilio, MSG91) — enter the provider details here once you have one. Until then, SMS attempts are logged only, not sent.
